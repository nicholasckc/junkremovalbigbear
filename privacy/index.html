What we receive

When you text, WhatsApp, call or email us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your name, phone number, the address or neighborhood of the job, photos of the items and your message.

How we use it

We use it to reply to you, quote and schedule the job, and follow up on work you book with us. We don't sell it.

Website analytics (no cookies)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to this website and see which pages are read. It does not use cookies or local storage, and Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors by IP address, browser or any other data. It records things like the page viewed, the page you came from, browser and device type, country and how fast the page loaded.

When you tap a text, WhatsApp, phone or email link, the page also loads a tiny blank counter page on this website (for example /go/whatsapp/), so we can count how many people get in touch. It does not record who you are or anything you type or send.

We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website. To opt out of the count, block static.cloudflareinsights.com in your browser or content blocker.
