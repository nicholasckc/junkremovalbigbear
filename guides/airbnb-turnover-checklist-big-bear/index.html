The part of turnovers your cleaner can't do

Big Bear Lake's vacation rental program has real enforcement: operational violations run $500 for a first offense, $1,000 for a second within 12 months, $1,500 for a third — and operating unlicensed starts at $1,500. Most of those violations trace back to the physical property: trash, parking, noise, occupancy. Your cleaning crew handles linens and surfaces; this checklist covers everything bigger. (Outside city limits — Big Bear City, Fawnskin, Sugarloaf — the County's short-term rental permit rules apply instead, including removing trash after each stay and using animal-proof containers.)

The between-stays checklist

  • Trash timing: containers out no more than 24 hours before/after collection — holiday-weekend overflow needs a pickup plan, not a prayer
  • Overflow bags: never stacked beside the bin (bears will find them before code enforcement does — and both are expensive)
  • Damaged furniture: photograph for the claim, then swap fast — a listing photo with a missing sofa costs bookings
  • Mattresses: replace on a schedule, not after the review that mentions the sag
  • Hot tub: drain-and-scrub on a cycle that matches occupancy, not the calendar
  • Deck & garage: clear of previous-owner storage and dead equipment before photography and license renewal
  • Guest-left items: bulky abandoned gear (broken sleds, coolers, chairs) out before the next check-in

Same-day windows are the whole game

Turnover work only counts if it fits between a morning checkout and an afternoon check-in. That's how we schedule: WhatsApp the job with your window, get a firm quote back fast, and the crew works inside the gap — lockbox or property-manager access, before/after photos when we lock up. Hosts and managers running multiple doors get priority scheduling and one point of contact for every property in the Valley.

The furniture swap play

The most efficient move for refreshes: schedule delivery of the new piece and removal of the old one for the same window. Old sofa out the day the new one arrives, packaging hauled — one visit, fewer days off-market.
