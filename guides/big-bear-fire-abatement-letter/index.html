What should I do if I receive a weed abatement notice in Big Bear?

Treat the letter as your official warning — Big Bear Fire Department is not required to send a separate violation notice before issuing a citation. Why you got the letter

Each spring, Big Bear Fire Department mails abatement notices to property owners across the Valley — occupied homes and vacant lots alike. It isn't personal, and it doesn't necessarily mean your property was flagged as a problem: the notice states the defensible space standards every parcel must meet before fire season peaks. What makes it serious is the fine print: the notification letter is your warning. The department is not obligated to issue a separate notice of violation before citing you.

What the Fire Department expects, in plain English

  • 0–5 ft from structures: no pine needles, forest litter, dead vegetation, or combustible mulch — bare, ember-resistant ground
  • Within 15 ft: dry brush and high-energy-release vegetation removed near structures, propane tanks, hydrants and wood fencing
  • Whole property: forest litter thinned to about 2 inches depth (a thin layer stays for erosion control)
  • Roofs, gutters & decks: pine needles cleared from roofing, gutters, under and on decking, stairs and parking pads
  • Trees under 45 ft: lower limbs pruned up to 6 ft (max 25% of tree height)
  • Trees over 45 ft: limbed 12–15 ft up, and nothing within 10 ft of chimney openings

The full standards are on bigbearfire.org, and the department offers pre-inspections for a small fee — call (909) 866-7566 to confirm current requirements for your parcel.

Your three options

Do it yourself. Doable for a small, flat lot — but budget full weekends, a truck for hauling, and transfer-station trips. The most common DIY failure isn't the clearing, it's the debris: piles left on the property don't count as abatement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the abatement letter a citation?

No — it's the official warning that precedes one. Big Bear Fire is not required to send a separate notice of violation before citing a non-compliant property, so the letter itself is your deadline.

Do vacant lots have to be cleared too?

Yes. Priority defensible space requirements apply to vacant and unbuilt parcels as well as occupied properties, and vacant-lot owners receive the same notices.
