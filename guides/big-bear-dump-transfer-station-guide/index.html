Where to Take Junk in Big Bear: The Complete Local Disposal Guide

Big Bear Transfer Station location and hours, curbside bulky-item pickup, bear-safe trash rules, and when a haul-away service beats a dump run. Local guide.

Where is the dump in Big Bear?

The Big Bear Transfer Station at 38550 Holcomb Valley Road (about 1.5 miles north of Baldwin Lake off Highway 18) accepts household waste, construction debris, yard waste, and tires — typically Monday–Saturday 8 AM–4:30 PM; call San Bernardino County at (909) 381-2404 to confirm hours and fees. For heavy or bulky loads, Junk Removal Big Bear offers pickup with free on-site quotes.

Option 1: The Big Bear Transfer Station

The Valley's public drop-off is the Big Bear Transfer Station, 38550 Holcomb Valley Road — about 1.5 miles north of Baldwin Lake at Highway 18. It generally operates Monday–Saturday, 8 AM to 4:30 PM (closed Sunday) and accepts municipal solid waste, construction and demolition debris, yard waste, and auto tires. Hours, fees, and accepted materials change, so call the county's line at (909) 381-2404 before loading the truck.

What a dump run really costs: a vehicle that can haul it, tie-downs, disposal fees, and the round trip up Holcomb Valley Road — plus your Saturday. For one load of loose bags, fine. For a couch, a fridge, and half a garage? Do the math first.

Option 2: Curbside programs

Curbside options depend on where the property is:

  • City of Big Bear Lake (92315): Big Bear Disposal gives each residence one free curbside bulky-item pickup per year (up to 3 cubic yards or 4 large items); extra pickups cost a fee. Call (909) 866-3942 to schedule. Bear-resistant carts are available for a one-time fee.
  • Big Bear City, Sugarloaf and Erwin Lake: the Big Bear City Community Services District (CSD) collects standard household bulky items curbside for a per-item fee, rents dumpsters, and holds periodic free community clean-up days. Call (909) 585-2565.
  • Clean Bear drop-off sites (41790 Garstin Dr and 39690 Big Bear Blvd) are only for residents and visitors staying in the City of Big Bear Lake, and only for bagged household trash and recycling — no bulky items, construction debris, or contractor loads.

Cart timing rules differ too: the City of Big Bear Lake prohibits leaving cans at the curb more than 24 hours, and the CSD allows carts out no more than 12 hours before collection and requires them back within 24 hours after.

Option 3: We come to you

For anything the free pickup won't cover — multiple items, heavy pieces, estate cleanouts, or the classic "the pile grew all summer" situation — a haul-away crew often makes more sense than people expect once dump fees, truck time, and a lost weekend are counted. Everything is loaded from wherever it sits (garage, deck, basement), and taken to a facility that accepts it.

What can't go in any of the above

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, motor oil, asbestos, propane tanks — can't go in curbside carts, our trucks, or regular transfer-station loads. San Bernardino County Fire runs household hazardous waste (HHW) collection; the Big Bear location is at the City of Big Bear Lake Public Works Yard, 42040 Garstin Drive, normally on Saturdays. Hours and temporary closures change, so check with San Bernardino County Fire's HHW program before you go.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Big Bear dump open on Sunday?

The Big Bear Transfer Station is typically closed Sundays, operating Monday–Saturday 8 AM–4:30 PM — confirm current hours with San Bernardino County at (909) 381-2404 before you go.

Does Big Bear have free bulky item pickup?

In the City of Big Bear Lake, Big Bear Disposal gives each residence one free curbside bulky-item pickup per year (up to 3 cubic yards or 4 large items) — call (909) 866-3942. In Big Bear City, Sugarloaf and Erwin Lake, the Big Bear City CSD picks up bulky items for a per-item fee — call (909) 585-2565.

Should I do a dump run or hire junk removal?

For single small loads, the transfer station is cheapest. For bulky, heavy, or multi-item loads, full-service pickup is often comparable once disposal fees, truck rental, and time are counted — and the free on-site quote makes it easy to compare before deciding.
