Where is the dump in Big Bear?

The Big Bear Transfer Station at 38550 Holcomb Valley Road (about 1.5 miles north of Baldwin Lake off Highway 18) accepts household waste, construction debris, yard waste, and tires — typically Monday–Saturday 8 AM–4:30 PM; call San Bernardino County at (909) 381-2404 to confirm hours and fees. Option 1: The Big Bear Transfer Station

The Valley's public drop-off is the Big Bear Transfer Station, 38550 Holcomb Valley Road — about 1.5 miles north of Baldwin Lake at Highway 18. It generally operates Monday–Saturday, 8 AM to 4:30 PM (closed Sunday) and accepts municipal solid waste, construction and demolition debris, yard waste, and auto tires. Hours, fees, and accepted materials change, so call the county's line at (909) 381-2404 before loading the truck.

What a dump run really costs: a vehicle that can haul it, tie-downs, disposal fees, and the round trip up Holcomb Valley Road — plus your Saturday. For one load of loose bags, fine. For a couch, a fridge, and half a garage? Do the math first.

Option 2: Curbside programs

If you have residential service with Big Bear Disposal, you likely get one free bulky-item pickup per year (up to about 3 cubic yards or 4 large items) — call (909) 866-3942 to schedule; extra pickups cost a fee. Two bear-country rules worth knowing: carts shouldn't sit at the curb more than about 12 hours, and bear-resistant carts are available for a one-time fee. Part-time residents and visitors can also use the Clean Bear drop-off program for bagged trash.

What can't go in any of the above

Hazardous materials — paint, chemicals, motor oil, asbestos, propane tanks — can't go in curbside carts, our trucks, or regular transfer-station loads. San Bernardino County runs household hazardous waste collection; ask when you call the county line, and we're glad to point you to the current option when we're on-site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Big Bear dump open on Sunday?

The Big Bear Transfer Station is typically closed Sundays, operating Monday–Saturday 8 AM–4:30 PM — confirm current hours with San Bernardino County at (909) 381-2404 before you go.

Does Big Bear have free bulky item pickup?

Residents with Big Bear Disposal curbside service generally get one free bulky-item pickup per year, up to about 3 cubic yards or 4 large items — call (909) 866-3942 to schedule.

Is junk removal cheaper than a dump run?
