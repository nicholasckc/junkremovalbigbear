What is weed abatement in Big Bear, CA?

Weed abatement in Big Bear is the required removal of hazardous vegetation — dry weeds, brush, pine needles, and forest litter — from private property to reduce wildfire risk. The Big Bear Fire Department mails annual abatement notices to property owners; failure to comply can result in citations. Weed Abatement — Frequently Asked Questions

When is weed abatement required in Big Bear?

Big Bear Fire Department mails abatement notices each spring, and hazardous vegetation should be cleared before summer fire season. The notification letter serves as the official warning before citation.

How much clearance is required around my Big Bear cabin?

Priority defensible space runs from the structure to 100 feet or your property line: an ember-resistant 0–5 ft zone, dry brush removed within 15 feet of structures and propane tanks, forest litter thinned to about 2 inches, and lower tree limbs pruned.
